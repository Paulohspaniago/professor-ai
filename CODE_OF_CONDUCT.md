# Code of Conduct

Professor AI welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Participation should make it easier for others to learn and contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.
- Discuss ideas and behavior; avoid personal attacks.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect others' privacy, attribution, and time.
- Help newcomers understand decisions and contribution expectations.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and moderation

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project publicly. The maintainer, [Paulo Paniago](https://github.com/Paulohspaniago), is responsible for moderation. Responses may include a request to change behavior, editing or removing content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban, depending on severity and repetition.

Moderation should be proportionate and consistent. Reports should be assessed in context, and private information should not be disclosed as part of enforcement. Retaliation against someone making a good-faith report is unacceptable.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** option on the relevant content or account to report abusive behavior privately to GitHub. For a non-sensitive moderation concern, you may also open a project issue and link to the relevant public discussion. Do not post personal information or private evidence in a public issue.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
